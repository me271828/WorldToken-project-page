WorldToken demonstrations

Watch WorldToken perform everyday kitchen tasks.

These 22 videos show the robot moving objects, opening drawers, and using kitchen appliances in simulation.

Each clip shows the same action from three camera views. The sentence above the video describes what the robot has been asked to do.
